Where the delay sits

Own loss is output a contractor's own machines, supply or clearance could not deliver. Starved is output lost because the stage before ran out of work, so it is not counted against that contractor. When drilling is blocked because blasted rock was not cleared, the loss is charged to loading and hauling.
