Plan arithmetic and assumptions
  • 30,000 BCM a day with 60 t of explosive is 2 kg per BCM, which matches the powder factor of 2. 1,200 holes a day gives 25 BCM and 50 kg of explosive per hole. Each shift plans 400 holes, 20,000 kg and 10,000 BCM.
  • Assumed, not given: tipper load 10 m3 loose, swell factor 1.3, 85% working efficiency, 16 shovels. The 5 minute cycle is read as shovel time per tipper loaded. With 5 tippers a shovel keeps working only while a tipper round trip is 25 minutes or less.
  • Cost of delay is assumed at Rs 150 per BCM not delivered. Change RATE in this file to your own figure.
  • Broken rock from one shift is available to loading in the next shift. The same holds for drilled holes and blasting.
